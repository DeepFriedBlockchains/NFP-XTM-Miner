XTM-MINER is an experimental NFProjects fork of CryptoSteer XMR Miner for Tari RandomX pools. It retains user-controlled foreground mining, native hash counters, CPU controls and Android thermal/battery safeguards. No application developer mining fee or reward redirection is used. Pool fees may apply. Upstream cloud accounts, remote control, updater and telemetry are disabled. Real-pool and physical-device validation are required before production release. See the repository validation report. Original source attribution and licenses are preserved.
